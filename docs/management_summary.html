What we found

  1. Freight is the largest adverse category: +€30,829 (+16.7%). June alone is +€21,340 (+44.6%), driven by one expedited air-freight line: €18,943 actual against €3,865 budget (+€15,077, +390%). Even excluding that line and its budget, June freight is still +€6,263 (+14.3%), so this is not only a one-off.
  2. Maintenance is second: +€25,776 (+16.5%). January accounts for +€14,081 (+61.7%), including an emergency chiller replacement at €15,115 against €2,775 budget (+€12,340, +445%). The description says “replacement”, which raises a capital-versus-revenue question as well as a cost question.
  3. Data quality is material to the numbers. Two exact duplicate pairs (€7,289), six mislabelled categories, three unbudgeted lines (€10,422) and four credit notes (€5,294) were identified. Payment duplication cannot be tested without payment records.
